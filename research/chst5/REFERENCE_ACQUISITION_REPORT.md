CHST bibliography, printed version **2021.06.09**. Listed titles, authors, editions,
years and publishers come from that attachment and its existing transcription;

HTTP status is unknown. No book, sample chapter, table of contents, product
description or licensing terms was retrieved or processed.

Their paths, parameter behavior and results could not be confirmed. They are

## Acquisition and rights policy

- Confirm the current BCSP blueprint and applicable reference list before selecting editions.
- Confirm a publisher or authorized seller has the listed edition or identify its successor; do not assume the newest edition is the BCSP-listed edition.
- Ask an authorized institutional library about licensed access, or obtain a legitimate publisher purchase/subscription where required. No institution-specific library access was confirmed.
- Obtain only official or otherwise authorized PDFs, samples, tables of contents and ebook access. A searchable title or citation is not permission to copy a book.
- Record actual permission and redistribution terms before uploading or exposing source text. An authorized copy for private review does not automatically permit public distribution.
- The report does **not** claim that all publications require payment. Payment requirements and prices are unknown; the BCSP Code of Ethics should first be sought directly from BCSP.
- Acquisition priorities below are provisional editorial triage based only on titles and the current curriculum gaps. They are not findings about chapter quality, current availability or verified objective coverage.
